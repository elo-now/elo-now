HOW DATA MOVES

Content stays encrypted
between your devices.

Messages are encrypted on your device, delivered through your team’s server and decrypted on participants’ devices.

An independent check on access.

A separate witness verifies signed access permissions for General channels, keeping authorization independent from message delivery.

Built for live collaboration.

Direct connections and media relays support voice and video sessions. Push notifications bring your attention back to new activity.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few practical questions.

Who is elo for?

Product teams, developers and organizations that want conversations, files and live sessions in one private workspace, with open source and a choice of hosting.

Can I host it for my organization?

Yes. Deploy elo on your infrastructure, or let 9bits handle installation, hosting and ongoing care for your team.

Can server administrators read our messages?

In current owner-managed Spaces, messages and attachments are encrypted for authorized conversation participants. Delivery and storage services handle encrypted content without receiving participants’ content-decryption keys. See our privacy policy and source documentation for the full architecture.

Do new members see earlier messages?

New members join General from the point they are admitted. Earlier conversation history stays separate from the invitation.

Does an expired server copy delete my local history?

No. The Space’s server lifetime controls delivery copies, while received history can stay on devices. Keep lets you set a separate expiry for individual messages.
