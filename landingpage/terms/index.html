Availability and backups

Delivery depends on network access, recipients’ devices, configured servers and operating-system notification policies. elo is not an emergency communication service. Spaces, conversations, messages and attachments can be removed by authorized user actions, account or Space deletion, retention rules, storage limits or technical failures. Deleted or unavailable content may not be recoverable. Save recovery material and, where needed, encrypted conversation backups. A recovery code alone does not restore history. Exported backups exclude attachment bytes and may omit older messages to stay within the displayed size budget; keep separate copies of files you need to retain.

Message and attachment availability

elo is not a permanent server archive. Server message lifetime offers 6, 12 or 24 hours, with 24 hours selected by default. This is the maximum delivery-storage period for encrypted message bodies in the Space, not guaranteed minimum availability. Copies may be removed earlier, including after an accepting device acknowledges direct-message delivery. Received messages can remain on members’ devices after the server copy expires. If you stay offline longer, missing history may depend on another authorized participant reconnecting with a retained copy; some messages may no longer be available.

The optional Keep setting is separate: No expiry, 1 hour or 24 hours, with No expiry selected by default and shown as ∞ in the composer. A timed setting controls when elo removes message content and shows Message expired in participating apps. Offline devices process expiry when the app runs again. Older versions may label this Delete after or retain a different previously supported deadline. No expiry does not create permanent server storage. Screenshots, exports and other independently retained copies cannot be recalled.

Attachments have their own download deadline, starting at upload reservation: 1 hour by default, with 12-hour and 24-hour options in Space settings for future uploads. Existing uploads keep their original deadline. New downloads stop at expiry; encrypted storage copies enter deletion, which can be delayed by provider or service failures. Already downloaded files can remain on participants’ devices. Save files you need before their displayed deadline; recovery codes and conversation backups do not restore attachment bytes.

These settings do not erase operational backups, access records, security logs or every independent copy at the same time. The privacy policy explains their separate retention and deletion criteria.

Owner-configured attachment storage

On supported versions and services, attachments start disabled. A Space owner can enable them during creation or later in Space settings by connecting a dedicated MEGA folder with write access or an S3-compatible bucket with restricted credentials. The owner must have permission to use the storage, provide access scoped to the intended location and comply with the provider’s terms. The separate storage broker holds and uses these credentials to transfer and delete encrypted files; the app API does not receive them in this flow. The broker does not receive the file-decryption keys.

Changing storage applies to future uploads. Existing objects retain their original provider and expiry. Disabling attachments stops new uploads but does not immediately erase files that remain downloadable until their deadlines. Older credentials are retained while needed for existing objects and their cleanup. Removing access at the provider can prevent both download and deletion. Provider quotas, outages, account suspension and loss of credentials can make files unavailable. Older Spaces may instead use publisher-configured storage.
