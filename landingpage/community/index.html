Community standards

A Space should be somewhere people can talk, plan and work together without harassment or abuse.

What is not allowed

Do not share child sexual abuse material, facilitate child exploitation or grooming, threaten people, encourage violence, harass or bully others, or promote hatred against protected groups. Non-consensual intimate imagery, impersonation, scams, malware, spam and unlawful content are prohibited. Respect intellectual property and other people’s confidential information.

Block a user

Use “Block user” from a message’s menu or Contacts to hide that person’s messages, reactions and direct requests on this device. Manage or undo blocks in More → Blocked users. Notification protection takes effect when the device confirms its updated policy with the notification service. Blocking does not remove a member from a shared Space; report abuse separately so its owner or operator can act.

Report a concern

Contact your Space administrator or elonow@9bits.com. Identify the Space, the user or content involved and the approximate time. Share only the evidence you choose and are entitled to disclose; do not send recovery secrets. If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services.

Protecting children

Child sexual abuse and exploitation are prohibited in every form. Do not circulate illegal material to report it. Provide a description or lawful reference and contact the relevant authorities where appropriate. Send child-safety concerns to elonow@9bits.com without forwarding illegal material. The publisher reviews reports and may refer suspected criminal activity to the competent authorities as required by law.

Operator responsibilities

Company Space operators are responsible for responding to reports, enforcing their membership policies and complying with applicable law. For publisher-operated services, send reports to elonow@9bits.com. Encryption is not an excuse to ignore a report made by a participant.

Enforcement

The responsible operator may investigate reports, restrict access and cooperate with lawful requests. Measures should be proportionate and respect the rights of those involved. Contact support if you believe an action was mistaken.
