Account & data deletion

Account deletion, local profile removal and leaving a Space are different actions.

Delete your account in the app

Open the account you want to delete, then go to More → Legal → Delete account. Read the confirmation, enter your profile password and tap Delete account. No email is required. If you have no Spaces and see Create a Space / Join a Space, open Legal from the upper-right corner.

The app checks your current Spaces. Primary ownership stays with the profile that created the Space and cannot be transferred. If you are a primary owner, open More → Spaces and tap the settings icon for that Space. Remove any other members in Members, then use Details → Delete Space. This removes the Space for everyone; tell its members before doing so. Then return to Delete account.

Once the connected services accept the deletion request, the app removes saved profiles and app data for this account from this device and signs you out. Server cleanup runs automatically in the background; you do not have to keep the app open. The app retains only an opaque, read-only receipt and confirms completion when opened online. Temporary service failures can delay cleanup; accepted work retries automatically. If a service cannot be reached, the app keeps the local profile so you can retry. A request already accepted by another service continues even if a later step fails.

On supported services, deletion removes membership and application contact details, attributed message and file ciphertext, associated delivery work and push registrations. Dedicated server snapshots of affected Spaces are removed. Minimal signed authority and revocation records and a deletion marker remain to prevent deleted credentials or content being reintroduced.

Request deletion without the app

For services run by the elo.now publisher, contact elonow@9bits.com with the subject “elo.now account deletion request”. State which Space or service you used and the public Identity ID, if you have it. If you do not have that ID, describe your account so support can help identify a safe verification route. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Never send a password, recovery words, protected recovery QR or private key. Support verifies requests proportionately and explains any records that must be retained, why, and for how long.

Data in a particular Space

To contact a Space owner about particular data without deleting your whole account, select the Space and open More → Legal → Request data deletion. Use draft prepares text addressed to the current primary owner; you choose whether to send it. Each primary owner must maintain a working contact address and can update it in Space settings.

Company-operated Spaces

If another organization operates the service, its deletion endpoint and policies apply. The elo.now publisher cannot directly erase an independent operator’s infrastructure. Contact that operator if its service is unavailable or does not support automatic account deletion.

Remove a local profile

More → Recovery → Remove from this device removes only the selected local profile and its saved keys and history. Other accounts remain. Signing out or uninstalling alone does not request server-side account deletion.

Disconnect one Space

In More → Spaces, confirm Disconnect for that Space to remove its conversations and contacts from this device and stop synchronization. Your account and other Spaces remain.

What can remain?

Other participants and offline devices may retain previously received messages. Files or backups saved outside elo and copies held by independent parties cannot be remotely erased. Old backups do not restore revoked access to our services. Additional operator-held support or legally required records follow the privacy policy and applicable retention obligations; contact elonow@9bits.com about those records.
