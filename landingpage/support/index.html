Support

A little help getting into the right conversation.

Contact support

elonow@9bits.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version and a short description of what happened. Never include your password, recovery words, recovery QR or private server credentials.

Join a Space

Open More → Spaces → Join a Space. Scan the invitation QR inside elo or paste its link. If approval is required, wait for the owner’s decision. Expired invitations need to be replaced by the owner.

Set up your own Space

Choose “Create a Space”, enter its name and a contact email for its primary owner, choose the available admission and message-lifetime settings, and share the invitation QR or link. The creator becomes its primary owner. Current elo hosting is free at launch and allows two Spaces per creator identity with 150 MB of encrypted message storage per Space and a separate 50 MB attachment quota. You can also connect to a Space prepared on an independent organization’s server.

In supported versions, choose Hosting when creating a Space. To use a private host, choose Add hosting, scan the administrator’s hosting QR or paste its link, review the public service details and add it. A private Space invitation may require this configuration to be added first. The hosting configuration carries public endpoints, verification keys and policies, not storage-provider credentials; get it from an administrator you trust.

Where supported, enable attachments during creation or in Space settings and connect a dedicated MEGA folder with a write key or an S3-compatible bucket with restricted credentials. The MEGA form does not ask for your account password. If the selected hosting offers managed storage, the app configures attachments during Space creation without asking for provider credentials; the operator configures those on its storage broker. Changing provider affects new uploads; existing files keep their original provider and expiry. Store releases and existing Spaces may offer a different storage model.

For deployment instructions, see the project documentation or contact support. Keep server and storage credentials out of invitation messages and support requests. A QR invitation should carry the invitation link, not the administrator’s private configuration.

Restore your profile

From the sign-in menu, choose Recover. Use your recovery code or protected QR. If you also saved an encrypted backup, use the backup restore flow to bring supported history back. Your recovery code alone restores identity, not a complete archive of old conversations.

Update the app

Install updates from the App Store, Google Play or the desktop releases page. If elo shows “Update required to go online.”, open the appropriate store or release page yourself, install the current version and reopen the app. You can still use saved chats and local features while online actions are paused. If the update is not available for your device or region, contact support. Do not uninstall the app to retry: local history may be lost. Keep your recovery material and backups safe.

Notifications

Open More → Settings and enable System notifications in the Notifications section, then allow them in your operating system. A muted chat does not produce message notifications or Buzz entries. System Focus, battery and notification settings can affect delivery. Reading a conversation allows its next unread alert to make a sound again.

Missing messages and expiry

Public elo.now hosting offers a Server message lifetime of 6, 12 or 24 hours, with 24 hours selected by default. An imported private hosting profile can also offer 48 hours or No expiry; the Space uses the policy selected at creation. Server No expiry retains encrypted bodies and locators after direct-message acknowledgement but does not remove quotas or prevent explicit deletion. Received local history may remain longer than a finite server lifetime. If a delivery copy is no longer on the server, Retrieve can succeed only if an authorized device with the required copy becomes available and synchronizes. Ask another participant to open and unlock elo; recovery is not guaranteed. Keep controls a separate message-content deadline: its No expiry (∞) choice does not change the server policy. Attachments have a separate 1-, 12- or 24-hour deadline, starting at upload reservation; the default is 1 hour. Backups follow separate operator policies.

Leave or remove data

Use Spaces to disconnect a Space from this device. To delete your account, remove only a saved local profile or request deletion without the app, follow the account and data deletion instructions.

Report abuse or a security issue

Contact your Space administrator or elonow@9bits.com. For a vulnerability, describe how to reproduce it using a test profile; do not include another person’s private data. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service.
